01 THE IDEA

Curiosity is
the starting point.

Orb Labs exists to explore what happens when a question is followed all the way to an experiment.

Not every project needs a customer, a launch date or a business case. Some are made to understand a system, test a limit, learn a technique or simply find out whether a ridiculous idea can work.

We build small proofs, measure what happens, record what breaks and share the result. A successful experiment might become a product. A failed one can still be useful knowledge.

03 THE KIND OF QUESTIONS WE LIKE

Example briefs.
Not project claims.

These are illustrative prompts inspired by the spirit of the lab. They are not published experiments, completed work or promises of future development.

EX / 001
CREATIVE COMPUTING · CONCEPT

Pixel Endurance

Give every pixel a limited transition budget. Once a pixel has changed state enough times, lock it to a warning colour. What patterns emerge as the budget runs out?

SIMULATION · CONCEPT

One-Tick World

What would it take to schedule updates for 69,000 world chunks in a single simulation tick? First measure the memory, scheduling and latency costs; then compare possible approaches.

The Unreasonable Question
